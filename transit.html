Public Transit

Arlington is one of the easiest U.S. cities to explore without a car. The DC Metro and bus systems branche out through Arlington, making navigation a breeze.

Key Metro Stations for Visitors

  • Arlington Cemetery
  • Rosslyn
  • Ballston-MU
  • Virginia Square-GMU
WMATA Metro System Map

Tips for Visually Impaired Riders

  • All stations have tactile paving near platform edges.
  • Frequent audible announcements and updates are made on the platform and inside metro cars.
  • Call WMATA or use the WMATA app for help planning routes and checking elevator status.
  • Service animals and visual aids are welcome.
  • Staff members are available at all stations.

Connecting to your Meals

All restaurants on our Food & Dining page are within a few blocks of Ballston-MU or Virginia Square-GMU stations, so you can grab dinner without a long walk.
